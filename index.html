Frequently Asked Questions

Do you work with businesses outside Port Talbot?

Yes. We are based in Port Talbot and regularly support businesses in Neath, Swansea, Bridgend and across South Wales.

Does my business need a risk assessment?

All employers in the UK must assess the risks to their employees and anyone else affected by their work. If you employ five or more people, the significant findings must be written down. We can carry out general, COSHH and fire risk assessments for you.

What qualifications do your consultants hold?

Our consultants are NEBOSH qualified and have over 30 years of practical health and safety experience.

Can you help with CDM and construction projects?

Yes. We help clients and contractors comply with the Construction (Design and Management) Regulations 2015, including preparing RAMS for specific tasks.
