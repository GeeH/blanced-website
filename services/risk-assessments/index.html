Risk Assessments in Port Talbot & South Wales

A risk assessment is a legal requirement for every employer. It identifies what could cause harm in your workplace, who could be harmed and how, and what you are doing to control the risk.

Blanced carries out practical, site-specific risk assessments for businesses across Port Talbot and South Wales. We visit in person, talk to the people doing the work, and give you a clear record of the risks and the actions needed.

What’s included

General risk assessments

Covering the day-to-day hazards in your workplace, from slips and trips to working at height and manual handling.

COSHH assessments

Assessing hazardous substances under the Control of Substances Hazardous to Health Regulations 2002, including exposure and control measures.

Fire risk assessments

A separate assessment of fire risks for your premises. More about fire risk assessments.

Task-specific assessments

For particular activities or equipment, which can be combined with a method statement as RAMS.

Clear action plans

A prioritised list of what needs to change, so the assessment improves safety rather than sitting in a file.

Reviews

Updating existing assessments when your work, premises or staff change.

What the law requires

The Management of Health and Safety at Work Regulations 1999 require every employer to carry out a suitable and sufficient assessment of the risks to employees and anyone else affected by their work. If you employ five or more people, you must record the significant findings.

Assessments must be kept up to date, so they should be reviewed whenever something significant changes, or after an accident or near miss.

Frequently asked questions

How long does a risk assessment take?

It depends on the size of your premises and the type of work. Call us to talk through what you need.

Do I need a separate COSHH assessment?

If your work involves hazardous substances, such as cleaning chemicals, dusts, fumes or solvents, you need to assess those risks under the COSHH Regulations. We can do this alongside your general risk assessment.

Can you review risk assessments we already have?

Yes. We can review your existing assessments, check they are suitable and sufficient, and update them where needed.
