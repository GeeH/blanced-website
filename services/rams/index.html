Risk Assessment Method Statements (RAMS) in Port Talbot & South Wales

RAMS (risk assessment and method statement) documents set out the hazards of a specific task and the safe way to carry it out, step by step. Principal contractors and clients often ask for them before you can start work on site.

Blanced writes clear, task-specific RAMS for contractors and businesses across Port Talbot and South Wales, so you can get on site with documents that reflect how the job will actually be done.

What’s included

Task-specific risk assessment

The hazards of the job, who could be harmed, and the controls in place.

Method statement

A step-by-step description of how the work will be carried out safely.

Equipment and PPE

The plant, tools and personal protective equipment needed.

Roles and competence

Who is responsible for what, and the training and qualifications required.

Emergency arrangements

First aid, rescue plans and what to do if something goes wrong.

Reviews of existing RAMS

Checking documents you already have before you submit them to a client.

Why RAMS matter

Risk assessment is a legal duty, and on construction sites the Construction (Design and Management) Regulations 2015 require work to be planned and managed safely. RAMS are a common way of showing a client or principal contractor that you have done this for your part of the job.

Generic RAMS copied from another job are a common reason for documents being rejected. We write RAMS around your actual task, site and team.
