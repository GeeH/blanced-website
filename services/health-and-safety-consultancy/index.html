Health and Safety Consultancy in Port Talbot & South Wales

Every employer in the UK must have access to competent health and safety advice. If you don't have that expertise in-house, Blanced can act as your health and safety consultant, giving you practical advice that fits the way your business works.

What’s included

Health and safety policy

A written policy that sets out your commitment, who is responsible for what, and your arrangements for managing risk.

Competent person support

Ongoing advice so you meet the requirement to appoint competent help under the Management of Health and Safety at Work Regulations 1999.

Risk assessments

General, COSHH and fire risk assessments, with clear actions to control the risks we find.

Compliance audits

A review of your current arrangements against the law and good practice, with a prioritised action list.

Accident investigation

Finding the root cause of accidents and near misses, and what to change so they don't happen again.

Training

Induction, role-specific and refresher training for your staff.

Frequently asked questions

Do I legally need a health and safety consultant?

You must have access to competent health and safety advice. That can be someone in your business with the right knowledge and experience, or an external consultant such as Blanced.

Can you provide ongoing support rather than a one-off visit?

Yes. We can provide ongoing support and be on hand when questions come up, as well as one-off pieces of work such as an audit or a set of risk assessments.

Which areas do you cover?

We are based in Port Talbot and cover Neath, Swansea, Bridgend, Llanelli and the rest of South Wales.
